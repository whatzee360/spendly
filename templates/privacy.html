Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, in order to provide the Service to you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate and improve the Service, including logging your expenses, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, authenticating your account, and communicating with you about your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to help operate the Service, such as hosting providers or analytics tools. These providers only have access to the information necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to protect your data. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.app.
